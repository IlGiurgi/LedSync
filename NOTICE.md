## Protocols
LedSync talks to devices through their public interfaces: the WLED JSON API, WLED realtime UDP (DNRGB) and
WLED Audio Sync v2 packets; the Yeelight LAN (inter-operation) protocol; the Govee LAN API; the Philips Hue bridge local REST API; ESPHome's `wled` light effect.
No code from WLED, Yeelight, Govee, Philips/Signify or ESPHome is included.
