# Privacy policy — LedSync

*Last updated: 1 November 2026*

LedSync does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Audio.** To follow the music, LedSync reads the audio that other apps are playing (Android's playback
  capture; Android shows a screen-sharing consent for this) or, if you choose, the microphone. The audio is
  analysed in memory, 20 ms at a time, to compute light colours. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  The screen is not captured: the consent is the one Android requires for audio capture.
- **Network.** LedSync only talks to the light devices you add on your local network (WLED, Yeelight, Govee, Philips Hue bridge, ESPHome),
  sending light colours and simple commands. It does not connect to the internet, has no analytics, ads or
  crash reporting.
- **Stored data.** Your settings (device addresses and names, effects, colours) stay on your phone in the app's
  private storage and are removed when you uninstall the app.
- **Permissions.** Record audio (playback capture / microphone), network access, foreground service and
  notifications (to keep running while the screen is off).

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
